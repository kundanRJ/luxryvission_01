Approach

Three questions before one swatch is chosen.

No mood board goes up until we can answer these for the room in question.

Where does the sun enter, and when. What does the body do here — sit, cook, sleep, pass through. And what does this room need to stop apologizing for.

Process

Three stages, one point of contact throughout.

The same lead designer walks the site on day one and signs off the last coat of paint.

01

Consult

We walk the space at the times of day you actually use it, talk through how you live, and leave with a brief — not a mood board.

02

Design

Material boards, floor plans, and a 3D walkthrough of the room before anything is ordered. Two revision rounds are built into every project.

03

Build

On-site oversight of contractors, joinery, and installation, with weekly photo updates so you're never wondering what's happening.
